How it works

  1. Connect a wallet on GIWA Sepolia (chain ID 91342). No funds leave your wallet until you confirm in your wallet.
  2. Pick a pair. Quotes come from the real Ammora SDK route client (@ammora-protocol/sdk@0.3.9) reading live on-chain state.
  3. Press Swap: the app prepares the exact-input swap, shows the approval + transaction, simulates it, then sends it only after your wallet confirmation.
  4. Paying with ETH? One click is enough: the app wraps the exact amount you need into WETH automatically as part of the swap — no separate step, no extra waiting.
  5. Need WETH without swapping? Wrap testnet ETH with the button below (calls WETH deposit()).
